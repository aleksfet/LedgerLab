Our Mission

This website is more than a simple teaching of accounting. It is not like textbooks or courses that may overcomplicate things or assume deep prior knowledge. Key areas of accounting are presented with clear, easy-to-understand explanations, with interactivity and outputs that show you how everything works. Whether you are a beginner or a business owner, building understanding through this website can go a long way.

Bank Reconciliation Helper

Compare your book cash balance to the bank statement, adjust both sides, and see whether they reconcile — plus the journal entries you'd record.

Use this when you have a bank statement for a month or statement period. This tool is about cash, not profit: it compares what the bank says your business has with what your business records say it has. LedgerLab then shows how accountants adjust both sides to see if the corrected cash balances match.

★ Use this when you have the bank statement for a month or statement period and your business's own cash records for that same period.

Depreciation Calculator

Straight-Line Depreciation, explained step by step.

When a business buys a long-term asset — like a van, computer, equipment, or furniture — the asset helps the business for more than one year. Depreciation shows how accountants spread the asset's cost across the years it is used, instead of recording the full cost as one expense right away.

This helps show how much of the asset's cost was used up during each year.
