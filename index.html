What LedgerLab Helps With

Six core areas of business accounting, each broken down into something you can practice hands-on.

📊

Business Budgeting

Plan income and expenses, set targets, and see how spending decisions shape a company's bottom line.

📒

Journal Entries

Record transactions the proper way with debits and credits, and learn why every entry has to balance.

🏦

Bank Reconciliation

Match your books against a bank statement, spot the differences, and prove that your cash balance is correct.

📉

Depreciation

Spread the cost of equipment and assets over time and understand how it affects expenses and value.

💵

Cash Flow

Follow money in and out of a business and learn the difference between profit on paper and cash in the bank.

📑

Financial Statements

Build an income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement, and see how they connect to tell one story.

Built for Beginners and Accounting Students

Most accounting resources force you to pick a side: oversimplified explainers that never show real numbers, or dense textbooks that assume you already know the language. LedgerLab does both at once.

Every tool gives you two things side by side — a clear, plain-English explanation of what's happening and why, and a clean, accounting-style output that looks like the real ledgers, statements, and reconciliations professionals use. You learn the concept and recognize the format at the same time.

  • New business owners — understand your own books with confidence.
  • Accounting students — connect classroom theory to real worksheets.
  • Curious beginners — start from zero, no prior finance experience needed.
